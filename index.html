What have you told your AI?

Memory Mirror reads your ChatGPT data export inside this tab and highlights the health, money, location, family, identity and secret details you have shared — so you can decide what to delete.

Drop your export here

ChatGPT .zip, conversations.json, memories.json, or a .txt of saved memories

  • Your export stays local. Analysis runs in your browser. The production policy blocks scripted connections (connect-src 'none').
  • No application database. Imported data stays in tab memory. Downloads remain on your device after you close it.
  • Masked by default. Values stay hidden behind a privacy screen until you reveal them.

Open your ChatGPT export

ChatGPT

  1. Request your export through ChatGPT's data controls.
  2. Follow OpenAI's export guide for your account.
  3. Download the .zip from the email and drop it here as-is.

Conversation JSON

  1. Prefer opening just the conversation file?
  2. Extract conversations.json from your ChatGPT archive.
  3. Choose that file instead of the complete archive.

Saved memories

  1. Open ChatGPT's memory settings.
  2. Copy the list into a .txt file.
  3. Drop it here together with the export.

Exports can take minutes to days to arrive. Memory Mirror only opens conversations*.json and memories*.json inside an archive; images and other files are never decompressed.

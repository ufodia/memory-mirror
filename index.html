What have you told your AI?

Memory Mirror reads supported LLM chat exports inside this tab and highlights the health, money, location, family, identity and secret details you have shared — so you can decide what to delete.

Drop your export here

ChatGPT or Claude .zip, Open WebUI .json, SillyTavern .jsonl, standard message JSON, or .txt notes

  • Your export stays local. Analysis runs in your browser. The production policy blocks scripted connections (connect-src 'none').
  • No application database. Imported data stays in tab memory. Downloads remain on your device after you close it.
  • Masked by default. Values stay hidden behind a privacy screen until you reveal them.

Bring your conversations into view

ChatGPT

  1. Request your export through ChatGPT's data controls.
  2. Follow OpenAI's export guide for your account.
  3. Download the .zip from the email and drop it here as-is.

Claude

  1. Request your export using Claude's privacy settings.
  2. Follow Claude's export guide for your account.
  3. Open the downloaded archive or its conversations.json file.

Other chat tools

  1. Open an Open WebUI JSON export or a SillyTavern JSONL chat.
  2. For other tools, use the standard message format.
  3. Copied memories or notes also work as .txt or .md.

Exports can take minutes to days to arrive. Memory Mirror only opens conversations*.json and memories*.json inside an archive; images and other files are never decompressed. Open other supported JSON and JSONL files directly.

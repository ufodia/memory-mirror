# Code of conduct

Memory Mirror is maintained in the open, and everyone who takes part is expected to help keep it a welcoming place.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient and assume good intent, especially with newcomers.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Share examples that are synthetic or anonymized. Never post someone else's personal data.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully and keep disagreements technical.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized content, doxxing, deliberate disruption and publishing private information without consent are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the [ufodia GitHub profile](https://github.com/ufodia). Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation to protect the community. Reports are handled confidentially.
